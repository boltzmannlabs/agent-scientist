# Common refinement decision guide — draft v1

Status: development guidance for **both** comparison arms. Not an installed
policy update, execution authorization, optimized algorithm or proof of benefit.
Freeze the version before future outcomes. The task's actual tools, public
parameters, limits and parent/selection rules govern.

## Goal

Improve complete eligible-panel numerical quality within the declared scientific
allowance. Treat mandatory eligibility, missing evidence and docking quality
separately. Do not pursue a secondary descriptor at the expense of the full
objective without recording that trade-off as an exploratory hypothesis.

## Before choosing the continuation

1. **Summarize the assessed evidence.** Keep all attempted selected identities in
   denominators, with invalid/unknown/missing/failure statuses. Report joint
   eligibility and overlapping constraint failures, not just marginal failure
   counts. Show how many otherwise eligible identities fail only each constraint.
   That count is diagnostic; it does not mean chemistry can change one descriptor
   independently while keeping all others fixed.
2. **Inspect the chemical deficit.** Compare measured candidate families and
   actual parent–product changes. Use existing QED, size, rotor, ring, polarity,
   charge, stereo and pose evidence. If QED components or structural alerts are
   calculated, retain the method and definitions. Do not claim a causal QED
   decomposition from a few correlations. Avoid spending new scientific
   evaluations merely to make the explanation more elaborate.
3. **Construct only supported options.** Consider STOP/final assessment and the
   task's eligible parent/library actions. A source's composition or name does
   not establish applicable fragments, smaller products or retained core/pose.
   No unsupported CReM count, radius, operator, seed or atom-mask parameters.
4. **Compare parents explicitly.** Include the task-permitted original lead and
   eligible generated alternatives. Weigh observed quality, constraint margins,
   molecular changes and uncertainty. The single best docking candidate need
   not be the best next parent; a high-QED parent can also have problematic
   undefined stereochemistry or weaker docking evidence. Keep units separate;
   do not invent an unapproved weighted reward or new hard cutoff.
5. **Choose a testable action or STOP.** Record the goal, evidence, alternatives,
   exact supported payload, reason, expected product-level observation,
   counterevidence and remaining allowance. If evidence cannot distinguish
   options, label a bounded choice exploration. All PAMPA labels passing is not
   a PAMPA deficit; low source LogP alone is not a joint-eligibility repair.

## After the continuation

Verify actual product changes, overlap and unexpected chemistry. Classify support,
counterevidence, no new information and unresolved observations separately.
Report joint eligibility, all failure bins and quality over the assessed cohort.
Distinguish standalone follow-up quality from a retained pool expanded by more
search opportunities. Tag finalists that had already appeared unassessed in the
initial emissions. No parent/library causal attribution follows from one batch.

Commit the task-defined ten slots before final calls, preserve missing slots and
failed observations, and apply the same selection/evaluation helper in both
arms. Scores remain predictions; raw measurement meanings, repeated-output
limitations and unknown resources stay explicit.

## Context and reuse

Keep a compact checkpoint: objective, frozen references/methods, candidate
summary, decisive exceptions, pending IDs, remaining counters, chosen action and
artifact pointers. Reuse validated immutable inputs and allowed existing
measurements; do not regenerate routine processing code each turn or inject all
raw files into every model call. Full selected skills/modules are already
supplied by the runtime. Do not bypass an unselected-skill refusal through a
filesystem read. Resolve missing required access before a live comparison.
