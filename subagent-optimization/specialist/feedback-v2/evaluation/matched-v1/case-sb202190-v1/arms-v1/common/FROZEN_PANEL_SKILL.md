# Frozen-panel molecular design

Use the current task definition, not a prior blocked-run report. Protocol revisions can change preparation authorization, constraints and whether numerical ceilings are required. Record explicitly unspecified limits when authorized; never invent ceilings.

## Preparation and resume

- Resume the exact durable run and accepted scientific job; never duplicate a pending or ambiguously accepted submission. Load the provider's complete matching contract before execution.
- Preserve earlier blocked records before updating outputs. Hash-check public inputs, fixed receptor, reference coordinates, scorer executable and descriptor assets before reuse.
- Verify target identity, author/label chains, construct mapping and missing coordinates. Keep protein-only generation pockets separate from prepared docking receptors and bound controls. Never supply a prohibited control graph as an editable lead.
- Investigate receptor aromatic typing warnings and verify atom identities and coordinate preservation; a successful docking call alone does not establish correct receptor preparation. Retain rejected preparations and count their readiness calls.
- Preserve partial-validation warnings and explicit user approval with the exact uploaded file hash. Connector acceptance is not complete scientific validation.

## Search and endpoint freeze

- Save episode allocation, selection and stopping rules before generation. Requested shape/decoding products are parameters, not guaranteed yields or observed raw attempts.
- Preserve all emissions, invalids, radicals, dummy atoms, disconnected species, duplicates and control recoveries. Separate raw strings/hosted properties from canonical identities/local descriptors. Do not equate hosted Lipophilicity with RDKit LogP.
- Use durable started/result records. Run long batches as tracked background terminal processes with completion notification because foreground tool ceilings can terminate a call. After interruption, inspect processes and artifacts before resuming; never automatically retry an ambiguous or exhausted attempt.
- Commit endpoint identities and panel hashes before final calls. Keep internal search scores separate from fresh final scoring and never replace endpoints after final results.
- Preserve unspecified input stereo, embedded configuration and pose identity separately. Verify molecular graphs and consistency with specified stereo rather than silently redefining input identity from a pose.

## Reporting and verification

- Require actual valid pose artifacts and finite coordinates; service Success or a numeric sentinel is insufficient. Retain raw scores and the exact derived transform.
- Calculate panel mean, median, sample SD, extrema and coverage. Missing slots or measurements make that metric's complete-panel aggregate incomplete; successful-only diagnostics need a denominator.
- Count setup, failed/interrupted, search and final docking attempts separately from descriptor scalars. Preserve provider billing units and inconsistent timing fields verbatim; unknown hidden draws, CPU time and reasoning costs remain unknown.
- Verify final-call timestamps follow commitment, panel hashes remain unchanged, pose identities match and summary statistics agree with top-pose files. Label execution-agent checks SELF_ASSESSED, not independent evaluation.
- Freeze reports, ledgers and hashes after workers exit. Preserve historical states/native outputs and inventory the final verification evidence without circular self-hashing.
