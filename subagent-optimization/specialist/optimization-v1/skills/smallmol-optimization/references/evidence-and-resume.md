# Evidence and continuation

## Identity and compatible comparisons

Use supplied exact-form IDs, original representations and method identities.
Do not merge unspecified and specified stereochemistry, salts, tautomers or other
forms without the task's explicit normalization rule. Keep the original reference
and generation parent as separate identities throughout the campaign.

Trust the host's calculated comparisons only within their stated scope. A local
evaluator reading the provider's output is not an independent predictive model.
Rounded hosted QED values do not replace an explicitly fixed unrounded local QED.
Different methods, physical units and qualifiers are not interchangeable.

All hard final criteria must be satisfied by the same candidate. Separate
feasibility from ranking; retain trade-offs as unsuccessful or unresolved when
appropriate. Do not invent a new weighted reward to turn failed hard constraints
into success. Counterexamples and negative outcomes remain part of the record.

## Waiting and recovery

Use the exact job name and document ID from the original connector receipt.
Pending/running are not failure. A status-observation error does not determine
the underlying job outcome. `Success` can mean computational job completion; it
does not prove the molecular objective was achieved.

After an ambiguous submission, preserve the original receipt and return waiting
or blocked with the concrete reconciliation need. Do not guess IDs, submit a
replacement, or reinterpret a timeout as confirmed nonacceptance. A host may
recover downloaded results locally without rerunning scientific generation.

If a downloaded artifact has not been assessed, report `waiting` with that
artifact reference and `awaiting_host_assessment` as the reason. Do not fabricate
new candidate properties or write observations into the environment to bridge it.

Continue only from an updated assignment/campaign snapshot. Carry forward the
original task, prior decisions, unresolved jobs and cumulative resources. Large
artifacts must be genuinely retrievable or summarized by the host; a hash alone
does not provide access to their contents. Ask for the specific missing evidence
rather than claiming to have inspected it.

## Reporting limits

Report actual scientific submissions, candidate rows, unique forms, cache reuse,
latency and available model usage separately. Unknown internal predictions,
credits, model versions or uncertainty remain unknown. Equal submission counts
do not establish matched oracle work. The environment's advisory checks do not
independently enforce execution limits or authenticate measurements.
