# Scientific reasoning principles — proposed contract

Tracking: agent-scientist#33. Drafted 12 November 2026 from the user's four
principles; reviewed by a separate evaluation critic. Status: **design addendum,
not installed or frozen**. It does not change the issued shared-preparation task,
allowances, saved specialist or historical records. Apply the agreed version
equally to both arms before a new comparison is frozen.

## Goal, options and choice

The goal is defensible molecular decisions with traceable evidence, visible
uncertainty and explicit trade-offs. Options were to add every endpoint now,
combine available numbers into one reward, or improve the reasoning/evidence
contract while keeping the first pilot narrow. Choose the third: current tools
and provenance do not support all requested physical endpoints. Adding columns
or a weighted score does not supply missing evidence. Broader objectives need a
separately specified task and qualified methods/data.

## 1. Evidence-aware decisions

Keep **evidence modality** separate from **origin**. A literature source can
describe an experiment, prediction or hypothesis; publication is not itself
experimental confirmation. Preserve existing outputs and link them through a
compact evidence index rather than forcing another output-format conversion.

| Information | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence ID and subject | Exact candidate/target/construct and molecular form |
| Modality | Experimental observation, model prediction, calculated descriptor, derived summary or hypothesis; unknown when not established |
| Origin | This run, supplied data or a specific literature/source record |
| Endpoint and context | Actual quantity, units/scale, conditions and preparation |
| Method/provenance | Available version, input hashes and job/artifact/row or citation |
| Status/dependencies | Successful, failed, unsupported or not measured; inputs/evidence and relevant limitations |

Vina Score and modeled contacts are computational outputs, not measured affinity.
PAMPA class 1 is a predicted binary label, not measured permeability or calibrated
confidence. QED and Crippen LogP are calculated descriptors. Native reference
coordinates do not make a generated analogue's pose experimental. Literature
target association does not establish activity of newly generated molecules.

For each consequential recommendation record the goal, supporting/opposing
evidence IDs, feasible options, selected action and reason, important uncertainty,
expected observation, evidence that would change the choice and remaining scope.
Do not relabel predictions as measurements of a physical endpoint.

## 2. Uncertainty has a consequence

Use a short record: **uncertainty → affected inference → decision consequence**.
Preparation, tautomer, stereo and method limitations travel with the observations
into parent choice and final interpretation. Weak target biology limits biological
prioritization and translational claims; it does not automatically invalidate an
unrelated descriptor calculation or authorize changing the task's fixed target.

State whether an uncertainty changes the action, limits the claim, motivates an
already permitted diagnostic, or remains unresolved within scope. Missing
mandatory eligibility evidence prevents confirmation. Unspecified stereo or an
unavailable worker revision does not automatically create a new exclusion.
Unknown evidence is distinct from demonstrated failure or an adverse observation.
Do not invent confidence numbers, numerical penalties, state enumeration,
rescoring or new rejection thresholds to make uncertainty look quantified.

## 3. Computational feedback now; experimental feedback separately

The current loop uses generated products, calculated descriptors, predicted PAMPA,
docking and failures. Call it **computational feedback**, not learning from genuine
experiments or updating model weights. Both proposed arms receive this feedback;
their comparison tests configured delegation, not feedback versus no feedback.

Record whether new evidence changes the action or supports retaining it, and why.
Changing a decision alone does not show better decisions; unchanged action may
be appropriate when evidence confirms it or is irrelevant. Receiving a result
and writing a new explanation alone does not establish adaptation or benefit.

A future experimental-feedback study needs genuine candidate-linked assay data,
conditions/controls/uncertainty, pre-feedback decisions and prospectively separated
with/without-feedback conditions. Match starting non-feedback information and
scientific opportunities; experimental-data availability is the intended
information difference. Distinguish retrospective supplied assays from newly
performed experiments. Evaluate downstream decisions against independently held-out
outcomes appropriate to the claim, with failures, coverage, resources and
prospectively fixed repeats/analysis. Outcomes available only for a fixed library
do not establish results for unsupported new compounds. Predictions cannot stand
in for missing experiments. This is a future design, not another arm or new assay
requirement for the present pilot.

## 4. Multiple objectives and visible trade-offs

Separate mandatory constraints, ranking objectives, supplemental descriptors and
missing evidence. Retain current PAMPA class-1, QED ≥ 0.5 and Crippen LogP 0–5
eligibility and complete-panel native Vina quality. This is a constrained
computational task; it does not cover the full drug-discovery objective.

Report existing quantities side by side with eligibility and constraint margins.
A more favorable docking score can coincide with lower QED, unresolved stereo or
other deficits. All PAMPA labels passing supplies no permeability ranking. LogP
has an allowed interval; decreasing it is not automatically an improvement.
More candidates/assessments increase search opportunity independently of decision
quality. Do not invent an unapproved composite reward.

Measured potency, selectivity, metabolism, toxicity, chemical stability and
synthetic feasibility remain not measured or unsupported here unless actual
authorized evidence exists. Neither docking nor QED substitutes for them. A
calculated SA score, when present, is a heuristic rather than proof of a viable
synthetic route. Unknown endpoints are not favorable zeros.

An optional non-dominated summary needs declared comparable dimensions,
directions and treatment of interval targets/missingness. It is supplemental,
cannot impute missing endpoints, and cannot change the frozen ranking or arm
adjudication. A broader multi-objective task needs its own evaluation contract.

## Review and scope

Review both arms for evidence traceability, unsupported claim conversions,
uncertainty-to-decision links, explicit trade-offs, meaningful feedback use and
preserved missingness. Keep these checks separate from molecular endpoint scores.
If made numerical later, freeze the rubric and denominators before outcomes;
do not retrospectively assign a new success score to completed runs.

The critic supported these amendments without new readiness jobs, endpoints or
changes to shared preparation. It specifically required matching initial
information rather than claiming identical information in a feedback ablation,
and preserving deterministic panel rules despite supplemental uncertainty.
