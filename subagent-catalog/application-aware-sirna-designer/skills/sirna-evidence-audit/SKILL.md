# Evidence and model-selection discipline

1. Freeze configs, inputs, model/checkpoint features and measured label/metric/split rules; retain every attempt. Previously examined labels are not an independent holdout.
2. Separate fresh inference, control replay, cache/identical-input reuse, author-score replay, metric reexecution and software probes. A shared-target application frame does not add an independent cohort or fit.
3. Block random learned features, dummy structures, truncated cardinality and fabricated context. A checkpoint load is not valid native inference; fixed seeds do not cure these defects.
4. Use genuine matched cohort/endpoint/protocol before reporting a numeric comparison delta. Clinical anchors and different publication cohorts are contextual evidence, not superior/inferior head-to-head results.
5. Retain RN.Ai as the historically selected incumbent when applicable; do not auto-promote the exploratory core19 setting. Block SiaRNA, ENsiRNA and BERT-siRNA until their actual prerequisites resolve. No substitute training under pretrained-only scope.
6. Apply the parent-supplied version-2 record auditor and opt-in artifact checks. Passing metadata/schema/hashes does not grant authorization or certify biology. Synthetic fixtures never enter measured benchmarks.
