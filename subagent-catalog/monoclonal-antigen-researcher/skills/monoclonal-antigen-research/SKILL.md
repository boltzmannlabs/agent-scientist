# Antigen research before workflow selection

Preserve supplied antigen identity, species, construct and antibody format. An antigen name is not a verified sequence or structure. Distinguish reference binder, design scaffold and assay control. Target-only research proceeds without inventing a reference; execution prerequisites remain explicit.

Read [the examination matrix](references/examination-matrix.md): identity, construct/sequence, topology, presentation, assembly, conformations, modifications, structures, epitopes, function/endpoint, specificity, variation, expression, known binders, assays/controls, reagent quality, biological relevance and gaps/conflicts. Prioritize findings that change the task's decisions; general questions need not trigger an exhaustive dossier.

Retrieve current primary records and literature. Record accession/version, source locator, date, species/construct, evidence basis and uncertainty. Keep experimental coordinates separate from predicted/repaired/cropped derivatives; preserve chain, sequence-to-structure and residue maps, pairing, assembly and glycosylation assumptions. Missing coordinates do not prove biological absence.

Distinguish supported, uncertain, conflicting, not-found, not-applicable and not-assessed findings. Negative searches retain scope; unavailable sources are not biological negatives. Explain implications for accessibility, valid tool input, control selection and measurable endpoints.

Return source artifacts and the concise dossier for independent review. Update consequential changed findings while preserving old versions. This is research; MAIN executes sequence generation, molecular modeling and scientific submissions. Read [the evidence procedure](references/evidence-procedure.md) for retrieval details. Permanent guidance contains no antigen, sequence, edit mask, candidate count or universal threshold.
